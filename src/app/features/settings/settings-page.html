Security & limitations

This app talks directly to your IPTV server from your browser — nothing is sent to any other server.

If you didn't choose "Remember me" at login, your password is kept only in this tab's session storage and is cleared when you close it.

Some movies/episodes are encoded as MKV, which most browsers can't play natively — you'll get an "open externally" option for those.
